Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Genie Auto Center ("we," "us") runs this website and our auto service shop. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to the records we keep when you're our customer.

Information we collect

WhatWhen
Name, phone number, email addressWhen you request an appointment, claim an offer, sign up for our emails, or become a customer
Vehicle details (year, make, model, mileage, VIN, license plate)When you tell us about your car or we service it
Notes you send usWhen you describe a problem or a request on this site
Service history, estimates, invoices, and paymentsWhen we work on your vehicle
Basic technical information (like your IP address and browser type)Automatically, when your browser loads this site and the services it uses (see "Services we use" below)

We don't collect payment card numbers through this website.

How we use it

  • To answer your requests and set up your appointments
  • To do the work on your car and keep your service history, which you can see in My Garage
  • To send service reminders
  • To send specials and coupons, if you signed up or are our customer. You can stop these anytime (see "Your choices")
  • To keep records the law requires of auto repair shops, and to protect against fraud
  • To see how our website and our advertising are working, and to show our ads to people who have visited this site

Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who run our shop software, website, and email for us, only as needed to provide those services
  • Advertising and measurement services (Meta Pixel), which receive information about your visit to this site and may receive a scrambled copy of contact details you type into a form. See "Services we use on this site" below
  • Anyone you ask us to share it with
  • Authorities, when the law requires it

Services we use on this site

This site loads a few things from other companies. When it does, your browser connects to them directly, and they may receive your IP address and browser information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps: the map on our page (Google privacy policy)
  • Google Fonts: the lettering on this site (Google privacy policy)
  • Unsplash: some of the photos (Unsplash privacy policy)
  • Supabase and GitHub: they host our site and securely store what you send us
  • TireConnect: the tire search and quote tool. What you enter there, such as your vehicle or tire size, goes to TireConnect (TireConnect privacy policy)
  • Meta Pixel, from Meta (Facebook and Instagram): measures how our Facebook and Instagram ads are working, and lets us show our ads to people who have visited this site (Meta privacy policy)

Meta Pixel learns which pages you view here and when you tap Call or Directions, look up an oil change price, or send a form. When you send a form here, Meta Pixel may also receive a scrambled (hashed) copy of contact details you typed, such as your email address, phone number, or name, which Meta can use to match your visit to an account you have with it. It doesn't receive your service history, your receipts, or the notes you write to us. The company that runs it may also recognize your browser or device, for example if you're signed in to its apps, and uses what it learns under its own privacy policy. See our cookie policy for the cookies involved, and "Your choices" below to opt out.

Your choices

  • Emails: every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. You can also email info@genieautocenter.com or call 619-312-2480 and we'll take you off the list.
  • See or correct your information: Email info@genieautocenter.com or call 619-312-2480 to ask what we have about you, or to fix anything that's wrong. Customers can also see their cars and receipts in My Garage.
  • Deletion: you can ask us to delete your information. We'll delete what we're allowed to. Some repair records must be kept by law.
  • Ads and measurement: you can opt out in Meta ad settings. You can also block or clear cookies in your browser's settings, or use a browser that blocks trackers. This site works the same either way.

California residents

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and share it, to ask us to delete or correct it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don't sell personal information. We do use advertising services (Meta Pixel) that receive information about visits to this site, so we can measure our ads and show them to people who have visited. California law may treat that as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, and you can opt out of it using "Ads and measurement" under "Your choices" above, or by contacting us. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We'll need to confirm it's you before we act on a request.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. There's no agreed standard for it, and this site doesn't change what it does when it's on. To limit tracking, use the options under "Your choices" above.

How long we keep it

We keep customer and repair records for as long as needed to serve you and as the law requires. We keep email signups until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

How we protect it

This site uses a secure (https) connection, and what you send us is stored with a provider that encrypts it and limits who can see it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date at the top.
